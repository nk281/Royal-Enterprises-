About Royal Enterprises

Design that's built to be lived in.

Founded in Delhi in 2016, we've grown from a two-person studio into a full residential and commercial design practice — without losing the habit of drawing everything by hand first.

Our story

Started with one renovation, and a stubborn floor plan.

Studio Arc began with a single apartment renovation in Lodhi Colony — a cramped, poorly-lit flat that needed more than furniture. It needed its floor plan rethought.

A decade on, that same principle drives every project: understand how a space is actually used before deciding how it should look. We now work across homes, offices and retail interiors throughout Delhi NCR.
