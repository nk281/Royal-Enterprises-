Services

Five ways to bring us into your project.

From a single-session consultation to a full commercial fit-out — pick the level of involvement your project needs.

01 — Residential Design

Full-home & single-room interiors

End-to-end design for apartments, villas and individual rooms — from layout planning to final styling and handover.

  • Space planning & layout revision
  • Material, colour & lighting palette
  • Custom furniture & joinery specification
  • On-site execution supervision
02 — Commercial Design

Offices, retail & hospitality

Interiors built for daily operational use — balancing brand identity with acoustics, traffic flow and durability.

  • Workspace & floor plan strategy
  • Brand-led material selection
  • Vendor coordination & fit-out management
  • Compliance & accessibility review
03 — 3D Visualization

See it before it's built

Photorealistic renders and walkthroughs so every material and layout decision is approved before construction starts.

  • Photorealistic still renders
  • 360° walkthrough animations
  • Material & lighting studies
04 — Space Planning

Get the layout right first

A focused engagement to solve a single problem space — an awkward layout, poor storage, or underused square footage.

  • Measured survey & existing-condition drawing
  • 2–3 layout options with trade-offs explained
  • Furniture & storage planning
05 — Consultation

One session, focused advice

A single 90-minute walkthrough — in person or virtual — for smaller decisions that don't need a full engagement.

  • On-site or virtual walkthrough
  • Written follow-up recommendations
  • Shopping & sourcing guidance
How it works

Our process, start to finish

Step 01

Consult

We walk the space, understand how you live or work, and set a scope and budget.

Step 02

Design

Layouts, mood boards and 3D renders are developed and refined with your feedback.

Step 03

Execute

We coordinate vendors and supervise on-site work against the approved drawings.

Step 04

Reveal

Final styling, a walkthrough with you, and a snag-list close-out before handover.

Not sure which service fits?

Tell us about your space and budget — we'll recommend the right starting point.
